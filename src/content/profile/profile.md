I'm a software engineer at **NAVER**, where I build the web crawling platform behind a search engine serving 4B+ queries a month. My work spans two sides of the same problem: collecting web documents reliably at scale in a distributed environment, and building models that understand what those documents actually contain.

Before NAVER, I was a machine learning research intern at the Computer Graphics and Applications Lab at Yonsei University. I led a project end to end — from model design and experiments to a first-author paper — on deep learning models that predict how deformable objects move over time, and designed a reinforcement learning-based algorithm for a paper published in ACM TOG (SIGGRAPH 2022).

I'm interested in how web-scale data becomes useful knowledge for AI systems — and in making those systems safe and reliable.
