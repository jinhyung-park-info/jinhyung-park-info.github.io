I'm a software engineer at **NAVER**, where I build the web crawling platform behind a search engine serving 4B+ queries a month. My work spans two sides of the same problem: collecting web documents reliably at scale on 1,000+ servers, and building models that understand what those documents actually contain.

Before NAVER, I did machine learning research at the Computer Graphics and Applications Lab at Yonsei University, which led to publications in ACM TOG (SIGGRAPH 2022) and on learning physical dynamics.

I'm interested in how web-scale data becomes useful knowledge for AI systems — and in making those systems safe and reliable.
