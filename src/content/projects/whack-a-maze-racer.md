Team project with Tian Yue Liu, Asjad Haq, Jenkins Tan, Aelea Ally, and Miaomiao Dong (CSC207, Fall 2019).

- Applied six design patterns (Factory, Observer, Facade, Strategy, Builder, Iterator) and SOLID principles for a maintainable, extensible codebase.
- Features: account creation and login, leaderboard and personal statistics, customizable game play, and mock in-app purchases.
- Was published on the Google Play Store (Canada).
